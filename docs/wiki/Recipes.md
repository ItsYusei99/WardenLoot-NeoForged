# Recipes

## Crafting

### Imbued Ingot

Place four Consumed Souls around an iron ingot:

```text
  S
S I S
  S
```

`S` = Consumed Soul · `I` = Iron Ingot.

### Warden Heart

Place echo shards in the four corners, Imbued Ingots on the four sides, and a diamond in the center:

```text
E I E
I D I
E I E
```

`E` = Echo Shard · `I` = Imbued Ingot · `D` = Diamond.

### Warden Blood

Place four glass bottles around a Warden Heart to craft four Warden Blood.

## Smithing upgrades

All upgrades use a **Netherite Upgrade Smithing Template**:

- Tools, helmet, leggings, and boots: matching netherite item + Imbued Ingot.
- Depth-Keeper's Heartplate: Netherite Chestplate + Warden Heart.
- Recharge a Still Heartplate: Still Heartplate + Warden Blood → Depth-Keeper's Heartplate.

The Still Heartplate is created when the charged Heartplate saves its wearer from death; it has no recipe to craft it from scratch.
