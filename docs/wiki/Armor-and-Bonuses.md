# Armor and Bonuses

The armor pieces use Imbued Ingots in smithing, except the Heartplate, which requires a Warden Heart. Values below are defaults and can be affected by configuration.

| Piece | In-game name | Default armor points |
|---|---|---:|
| Helmet | Depth-Keeper's Helmet | 8 |
| Charged chestplate | Depth-Keeper's Heartplate | 14 |
| Leggings | Depth-Keeper's Greaves | 11 |
| Boots | Depth-Keeper's Boots | 8 |
| Uncharged chestplate | Still Heartplate | 8 |

Charged pieces use 4 toughness and 0.15 knockback resistance by default. The Still Heartplate has half the toughness and 0.05 knockback resistance.

## Helmet

The Depth-Keeper's Helmet prevents the wearer from receiving the Darkness effect.

## Full set: Scourge of the Depths

When the configured full set is equipped, the Warden cannot keep the player as its target; the mod clears its anger toward that player and resets its dig cooldown. Which pieces count depends on the armor options in the config.

## Heartplate lifesaving effect

If a charged Heartplate is equipped and its wearer takes fatal damage that does not bypass invulnerability, the chestplate:

1. Turns into an uncharged **Still Heartplate**.
2. Leaves the player at 1 health and cancels the death.
3. Clears all active effects, then grants Regeneration II for 45 s, Absorption II for 5 s, Fire Resistance for 40 s, and Darkness for 30 s.

The Still Heartplate is obtained this way; there is no initial crafting recipe for it. Recharge it with a Netherite Upgrade Smithing Template and Warden Blood in a smithing table.
